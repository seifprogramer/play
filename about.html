About the game

Ringshift

Ringshift is a small, free browser arcade game built around a single idea: three rings spin around a central core, each seeded with drifting hazards, and you have exactly one move — shift outward to the next ring. Survival comes down to timing, not reflexes alone.

How it plays

  • Tap the screen, click, or press space to shift to the next ring out.
  • Red hazard arcs rotate around each ring at different speeds — get caught under one and you lose a life.
  • Gems drift past the top marker too. Catch them for bonus points.
  • The rings speed up the longer you survive, so the pace keeps climbing.

Why we built it

Most arcade games ask for a lot of buttons. We wanted something that fit in one input and still felt tense — closer to a rhythm game than a shooter. Ringshift is the result: no menus to dig through, no tutorial longer than three lines, just rings and timing.

Who's behind it

Ringshift is an independent project from Orbit Labs, a small team making short, replayable browser games. The game is free to play, runs entirely in your browser, and doesn't require an account.
